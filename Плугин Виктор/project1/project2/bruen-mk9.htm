Bruen Mk9

Details
Blueprints and Attachments in Warzone MW3

The Bruen Mk9 is a weapon featured in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 3 (2023), it is one of the LMGs available in the game.

The Bruen Mk9 will be available in Modern Warfare 3 with the launch of the game on November 10, 2023, and on Warzone later this year with Season One.

The design of the Bruen Mk9 is based the real-life gun FN Minimi.

Here you can find the main information about the weapon in MW3, including all the unlockable attachments and the complete Weapon Blueprints list for the Bruen Mk9 available in COD Modern Warfare 3 and COD Warzone.

Bruen Mk9
The Bruen Mk9 is a fully automatic, air-cooled light machine gun chambered in 5.56. Support your team and pin down the enemy with a high fire rate and overwhelming covering fire.
