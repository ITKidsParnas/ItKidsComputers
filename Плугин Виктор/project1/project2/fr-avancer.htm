FR Avancer

Details
Blueprints List in Warzone and MW2

The FR Avancer is a weapon featured in Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2 (2022), it is one of the Assault Rifles available in the game.

The FR Avancer will be added to the game as part of the free content included in Season 5.

The design of the FR Avancer is based the real-life gun FAMAS Felin.

Here you can find the main information about the weapon, the best FR Avancer loadout for the MW2 multiplayer and Warzone, and the complete Weapon Blueprints list for the FR Avancer available in COD Modern Warfare 2 and COD Warzone 2.

FR Avancer
With a blinding fire rate and exceptional maneuverability, in the right hands, this aggressive bullpup rifle can dominate the battlefield.
